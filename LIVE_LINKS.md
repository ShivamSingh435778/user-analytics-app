# 🔥 User Analytics & Heatmap Tracker

**Live:**
- Demo: https://user-analytics-app-pxav.onrender.com/demo/

**Stack:** Node.js, Express, MongoDB, Vanilla JS Tracker

**Features:**
- ✅ Real-time click tracking (x,y coordinates)
- ✅ Session ID tracking
- ✅ Density-based Heatmap (yellow → red)
- ✅ REST API: GET /api/events?limit=, GET /api/heatmap?url=
- ✅ Clear Data for Demo

**How to test:**
1. Go to /demo and click anywhere
2. Open /dashboard.html and see heatmap live
